# **App Name**: Harvest Recipes AI

## Core Features:

- Ingredient Photo Upload: Users can upload one or more photos of their available ingredients. The system will process these images to identify relevant items.
- AI Recipe Suggestion Tool: Leverages AI to generate novel recipe suggestions, complete with estimated preparation times, difficulty levels, and nutritional information, based on the identified ingredients and dietary preferences. The AI uses reasoning to propose the most suitable dishes.
- Detailed Instruction Generation: For each suggested recipe, the AI tool provides clear, step-by-step instructions and a list of all required ingredients, distinguishing between user-provided and missing items.
- Recipe Browsing and Viewing: A user-friendly interface to browse suggested recipes and view their details, including ingredients, instructions, and nutritional facts.
- Favorite Recipe Saving: Users can save their preferred recipes to a personalized collection for easy access in the future. Recipes will be stored securely.
- User Recipe Collection: A dedicated section where users can view and manage their saved recipes, allowing for quick retrieval and review.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary brand color: A sophisticated, earthy green (#305526), reflecting natural ingredients and wholesome cooking, used for primary elements and key accents.
- Background color: A very subtle, nearly off-white with a hint of green (#EBEDEA), providing a clean and inviting canvas for content, ensuring readability in a light theme.
- Accent color: A fresh, bright chartreuse (#BFD868), offering a lively contrast for interactive elements and highlights, bringing a modern and energetic feel.
- Headline font: 'Playfair' (serif) for an elegant, high-contrast, and sophisticated feel, enhancing the presentation of recipe titles.
- Body font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for excellent readability and a modern, friendly touch, suitable for detailed recipe instructions and descriptions.
- Use line-art style icons related to cooking, food preparation, and ingredients, maintaining a clean and minimalist aesthetic.
- Clean, grid-based layout for ingredient display and recipe suggestions, with ample whitespace to ensure focus and easy navigation.
- Subtle fade-in and slide-up animations for newly loaded recipe cards and modal transitions, providing a smooth and engaging user experience.
